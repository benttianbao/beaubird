# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BeauBird is a private, authenticated workspace for its owner, an active birder who uses it on a desktop at home or while planning and reviewing field activity. Mobile access will follow, so workflows must remain structurally responsive even when desktop is the first delivery target.

## Product Purpose

BeauBird brings bird-record lookup, preparation, monitoring, personal life-list gaps, and spatial exploration into one focused workspace. The map feature must answer two complementary questions: where the owner has seen birds across all personal history in China, and which birds were recently recorded at Zhejiang observation points during the two years ending at the dataset cutoff. Success means the owner can move the map, inspect nearby birding points, read the latest records and recently observed species, reverse-filter the map by species, and open an unlocked species directly on the map.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, natural. The interface should feel like a dependable field notebook and research workbench: quietly confident, information-dense when needed, and never ornamental at the expense of clarity.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a consumer navigation app, a social feed, a generic analytics dashboard, or an AI-generated card gallery. Avoid decorative gradients, glass effects, oversized metrics, novelty controls, map clutter, and attention-seeking rare-bird styling. Do not expose raw observation archives, hidden locations, observer identities, or sensitive species locations.

## Design Principles

1. Put the map and the current geographic question first; controls should support the visible area instead of competing with it.
2. Reveal detail progressively: clustered places first, recent records and recent species on selection, full history only when explicitly requested.
3. Make data provenance and time cutoffs visible so historical evidence is never mistaken for live data.
4. Preserve privacy by architecture: authenticated access, derived datasets, minimal API fields, and no raw archive delivery to the browser.
5. Connect existing workflows instead of duplicating them; monitoring, BirdReport results, and unlocked species should enter the same map state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. All controls and point-detail interactions must be keyboard accessible, retain visible focus, expose meaningful labels and live status, avoid color-only distinctions, respect reduced-motion preferences, and remain usable at 320px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px widths.
